## 🚀 Featured Work

### 🥇 RentAll BD

**A Bangladesh-focused peer-to-peer rental platform concept.**

RentAll BD explores how people in Bangladesh could **rent out and rent tools, equipment, and other useful items through a structured digital marketplace**.

My work focused on turning the initial concept into something more practical for the Bangladeshi context.

**My contribution included:**

* Product concept development
* Understanding the local problem and market context
* Designing the rental workflow
* Thinking through how the platform could operate
* Product presentation and pitching

🏆 **1st Runner-Up — StartUp InnovateX**

> One of the projects that taught me that having an idea is easy — making the idea workable is the real challenge.
